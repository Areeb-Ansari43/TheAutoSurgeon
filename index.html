GETTING HERE

Easy to find. Easier to park.

By car

Off the A312 Parkway, just south of Hayes town centre. Nearest motorway: M4 J3.

Free on-site parking at Squirrels Trading Estate.

By rail

Hayes & Harlington on the Elizabeth line — short ride from central London and Heathrow.

~15 min walk, or a short bus / cab from the station.

By bus

Routes 90, 140, 195, U4, U5, E6, H98 stop on North Hyde Road, a few minutes’ walk from Viveash Close.

Night bus: N140.

By tube

Nearest tube is Hounslow West on the Piccadilly line. Best paired with a short bus to North Hyde Road.

Heathrow terminals: short drive away.
